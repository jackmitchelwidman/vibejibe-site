VibeJibe Waiting list

Privacy

Last updated November 9, 2026

Who we are

VibeJibe is operated by Jack Widman, who does business under the name ChocolateLabs. To reach us about anything on this page, write to woof@chocolatelabs.me.

What this page covers

VibeJibe has not opened yet. Today this website does one thing: it lets you join the waiting list. This page covers that. Before the clubs open we will publish a full policy for the service, and we will tell everyone on the waiting list about it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, if you type it into the waiting list form.
  • Your ZIP code or city, which you type into the same form.
  • The date and time you joined the list.
  • How you arrived, when the link you followed says so: a short tag that names where the link was posted, or the share link of the person who sent it to you. We keep the tag, and a note of whose link it was.
  • Visit statistics, described under "Counting visits" below.

The form asks for nothing else. It has no name, street address, phone number or password.

What we use it for

We use your email address to send you two emails. The first is sent when you join: it confirms you are on the list and gives you a link of your own that you can send to friends. The second tells you that the clubs are open where you live and how to get in. We may also write to tell you that you can create your VibeJibe before opening night. Every email has a link that takes you off the list.

If you joined before November 9, 2026, the confirmation email and the share link were not part of what this page said then, so we did not send them to you.

When someone joins through your link, we count it. The website shows you how many New Yorkers your link has brought. It shows a number only. The person who sent you a link is not told that you joined, and we never write to anyone because a friend gave us their address.

Your browser keeps three small notes on your own device so the page works when you come back: the tag or share link you arrived by, your own share link once you join, and whether you gave a New York place. They are not cookies and are not sent to anyone but our own server when you join.

We use your ZIP code or city to know which city you are waiting for. The website shows how many people in New York are on the list. It shows only that number, never a name, an email address or a place.

We do not sell it. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not send you marketing from anyone else.

Where it is kept

The waiting list is stored on a server at Amazon Web Services in Sweden. A backup copy of the server's data is made each night and stored with the same company in the same country.

Other companies involved

  • Amazon Web Services runs the server that stores the waiting list, and delivers the emails we send to it.
  • GitHub hosts this website. Like any web host, it receives your IP address, which is the number that identifies your internet connection, when you load a page.
  • Google Fonts supplies the lettering on this website. Your browser fetches the fonts from Google, so Google also receives your IP address.
  • Google Analytics counts visits to this website, as described next.

Counting visits

We use Google Analytics to count how many people visit this website, which pages they read, roughly where in the world they are, and whether they joined the waiting list. Google Analytics sets cookies, which are small files your browser keeps, so that it can tell a returning visitor from a new one. Google receives this information and processes it for us. Its advertising features are turned off. Your email address is never sent to Google Analytics.

  • If you are in Europe, the website asks you first. Nothing is counted and no Google Analytics cookie is set unless you click Allow. Your answer is remembered for one year in a cookie named vj_analytics.
  • If you are anywhere else, your visit is counted unless you turn counting off.
  • To turn counting off, switch on Global Privacy Control in your browser. It is a browser setting that tells websites not to track you, and this website obeys it. You can also install Google's opt-out add-on.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until the clubs open and we have written to you, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first. If you then create a VibeJibe account, the full policy for the service applies to that account.

Leaving the list

Every email we send to the list ends with a link. Open it, press the button, and your address is deleted from the list. You can also write to woof@chocolatelabs.me from the address you joined with and ask to be removed, and we will delete it. It may remain in earlier nightly backups until those backups are deleted.

You can also write to the same address to ask what we hold about you or to have it corrected. If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, these are your rights under data protection law, and you may also complain to your national data protection authority.

Age

VibeJibe is for adults. Please do not join the waiting list if you are under 18.

Changes

If we change this page, we will change the date at the top. If the change affects how we use an email address already on the list, we will write to that address before the change takes effect.
