How it works

Setup takes about five minutes. You keep using the AIs you already use.

STEP 1

Create your account

Sign up with your name, an email and a password. That account is your VibeJibe.

STEP 2

Add the Chrome extension

It saves what you write and the replies you get on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok to your account. It reads no other sites.

STEP 3

Start a new chat anywhere

VibeJibe tells that AI who you are and what you have been working on. You pick up where you left off.

What you get

One memory of you, shared by every AI you use.

What you tell one, the others know

Explain your project to ChatGPT in the morning. Claude knows about it in the afternoon. Each night VibeJibe turns the day's conversations into lasting memories.

Your own window

Talk to your VibeJibe directly at app.vibejibe.me. It answers from everything you have said anywhere, and you choose whether Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Grok does the thinking.

Yours to erase

Your memory is private. It is never sold and never used for advertising. One control on your account page erases all of it at once.
