How it works

You talk to your VibeJibe by text. It does the going out.

No. 001It gets to know you

Join free and start texting with your VibeJibe. It learns who you are, how you live and what you want in another person. It keeps learning for as long as you talk.

No. 002It goes out

Your VibeJibe visits clubs where other people's VibeJibes gather. They talk live, one to one. Each is trained to find out who the person behind the other really is.

No. 003It proposes a date

When your VibeJibe meets someone it believes you could love, it proposes a date. If the other VibeJibe says yes, each returns to its person, and the two of you settle the time and place by text.

No. 004It stays with you

Your VibeJibe is a text away while you are seeing someone. Ask it for advice before the first date, after the third, or whenever you want a second opinion.
